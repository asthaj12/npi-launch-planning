Assumptions & Limitations
  • All NPI program data and product identifiers are synthetically generated; no employer-specific program data is used.
  • CTB rates, lead times, and supplier tiers are illustrative; production systems integrate live ERP and procurement data feeds.
  • E&O risk estimates use simplified residual inventory × margin; actual models account for channel mix and salvage value.
  • Supplier risk scores are constructed from public supply-chain risk frameworks and do not reflect any specific company's assessments.
  • S&OP gap and accuracy metrics are illustrative targets; production systems use SKU-level statistical forecasting.
