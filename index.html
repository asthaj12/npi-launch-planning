⚠ DEMO DATA — Illustrative numbers only. Synthetic demo · Multi-hundred-million-dollar NPI portfolio · Fixed-window device launches · Regional GTM across thousands of locations
Decision
How do we protect fill rate and revenue on fixed-window hardware launches when DC allocation, ODM delivery, and regional GTM operate across independent teams without direct authority?
Evidence
Launch-week fill rate improved from ~61% to ~84% on fixed-window device launch. Fixed windows have no markdown path — every miss is permanent revenue loss. Import commitments are 26-week irreversible decisions.
Recommendation
Gate-based allocation model: lock DC commitments at T-4 weeks, trigger weekly fill rate pacing protocol, escalate at-risk regions at T-2. Prevents relying on launch-week data — by then it's too late to act.
Primary Risk
Regional DC capacity constraints in high-volume districts depress fill rate below 75% even with correct total allocation volume. Early-signal monitoring is the only lever once the launch window opens.
